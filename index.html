Common questions

A client paid for several invoices at once. How do I record it?

Open the client and tap Record payment. Enter the amount received and Taqaza splits it across their open invoices, oldest first. Switch to Manual to choose the amount for each invoice yourself. Anything left over is kept as the client's credit.

The client deducted income tax before paying.

Enter the cash in Amount received and the deduction in Tax withheld by client. Both count toward the invoices.

A cheque bounced.

Open the payment and tap Reverse payment. The invoices it paid become due again, and the payment stays on the statement, struck through.

A job was misprinted or returned.

Open the invoice and tap Issue credit note. It reduces what the client owes and can be shared as a PDF. If the invoice was already paid, the amount becomes the client's credit.

How do I move my records to a new phone?

On the old phone, go to Settings → Save a copy to phone storage and send that file to the new phone (for example by WhatsApp or email). On the new phone, install Taqaza and use Settings → Restore from a backup file.

Are my records uploaded anywhere?

No. Taqaza has no account and no server; your records stay on your phone. See the privacy policy.

The app lock does not recognise my fingerprint.

Choose the option to use your phone's PIN, pattern or passcode in the unlock prompt. You can turn the lock off in Settings → Security.
