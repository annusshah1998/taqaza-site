Privacy policy

Taqaza keeps your business records on your phone. We do not collect, store or share any of your data.

Effective 16 October 2026. This policy covers the Taqaza app for Android and iPhone.

What Taqaza stores, and where

Taqaza stores the records you enter only on your own phone, in the app's private storage:

  • your business profile: name, address, phone, email, NTN, STRN, bank details, logo and signature image;
  • your clients: business names, contact people, phone numbers, addresses, NTN and STRN;
  • invoices, payments, tax withheld, credit notes, write-offs and app settings.

What we collect

Nothing. Taqaza has no account or sign-up, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send your records over the internet, and we cannot see them.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to send it. This happens when you:

  • share an invoice, credit note or statement as a PDF, or copy a statement as text;
  • save a backup copy to a folder or app you pick.

The person or app you share with (for example WhatsApp, email or a cloud drive) handles that copy under their own terms.

Backups

Taqaza saves a backup file once a day in its private storage on your phone and keeps the newest 10. Copies you save to phone storage or elsewhere are under your control.

Permissions

  • Photos: only to pick the logo or signature image you choose for your invoices.
  • Files: to save backup copies and to open a backup file you choose for restoring.
  • Fingerprint or Face ID: only if you turn on the app lock. Your phone's system checks it; Taqaza never receives your fingerprint or face data.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Taqaza deletes everything in the app, including its daily backups. Delete any backup copies you saved elsewhere yourself.

Children

Taqaza is a business tool and is not intended for children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
